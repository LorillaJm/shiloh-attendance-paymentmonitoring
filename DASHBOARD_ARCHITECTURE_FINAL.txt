================================================================================
KEY SUCCESS FACTORS
================================================================================

1. ✅ Single query per widget (subqueries)
2. ✅ All queries use indexed columns
3. ✅ Aggressive caching (3-5 min TTL)
4. ✅ No auto-polling (manual refresh only)
5. ✅ Limit results to 10 rows max
6. ✅ No heavy relationship loading
7. ✅ Scheduled cache warmup (3x daily)
8. ✅ Selective cache invalidation
9. ✅ COUNT(*) instead of model loading
10. ✅ Composite indexes for multi-column queries
